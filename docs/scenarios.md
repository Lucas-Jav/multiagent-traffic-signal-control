# Experimental Scenarios V1

All V1 scenarios use the same versioned 3x3 SUMO network:
`scenarios/grid_3x3/network.net.xml` (one lane per approach, 235.6 m internal edges, 92.8 m
border edges, one approach served at a time by the 98 s fixed-time program).

The scenario YAML is the source of truth for:

- simulation seed;
- route file name;
- demand profile and demand level;
- turning ratios (scenario level and optional per-entry override);
- demand schedule;
- border entries, their rates and time windows;
- the S3 shock time (`traffic.shock_start_seconds`).

Scenario files are generated, not hand-edited:

```powershell
uv run python scripts/generate_scenarios.py
```

The script (`scripts/generate_scenarios.py`) contains the S1-S4 definitions, writes the
per-seed YAMLs and route artifacts for the fixed V1 seed pool (`42, 2027, 3253, 4517, 5791`)
and writes the seed-42 file as the scenario's base file.

Controllers must not receive scenario-specific configuration. A controller may be selected at
run time with `--controller`, but the scenario files do not define special controller
parameters.

The demand intensities below were CALIBRATED in Prompt 46.7 with controller-independent criteria
(`scripts/calibrate_scenarios.py`, `B0_FIXED` only, calibration seeds `101, 1009, 2029`, which
are disjoint from the fixed seed pool). Each level is the highest candidate of a predeclared
grid that satisfied every predeclared criterion on all calibration seeds; the criteria, the
candidate results and the selection are in `artifacts/calibration/pilot_v2-scenario-calibration/`
(`calibration-plan.md`, `candidate-results.parquet`, `selected-scenario-levels.md`). Calibration
is scenario calibration, not controller tuning, and is not a scientific result.

## Demand model

`traffic.entries` lists border entry edges (`left0A0`, `left1A1`, `left2A2`, `right0C0`, ...,
`bottom2C0`), each with a rate per demand level (`LOW`/`MEDIUM`/`HIGH`), a time window in
absolute simulation seconds and a `group` (`base`, `dominant`, `residual`, `shock`, `hotspot`).

Vehicles are generated by a seeded, deterministic junction-turning walk
(`src/traffic_agents/simulation/route_walk.py`): departures are evenly spaced at the entry rate
(seeded phase); at every signalized junction a vehicle chooses `straight`/`left`/`right` with
the configured turning ratios until it leaves through a border exit edge. Only legal movements
of the network are used; illegal turns are never forced. An entry may set
`via_intersection_id`: its vehicles first follow a shortest legal path to that intersection
(used for the S3 shock and the S4 hotspot) and then continue with the turning-ratio walk.
The turning ratios are therefore operational: they shape the realized routes.

The route file lists explicit `<vehicle>` elements sharing `<route>` definitions; vehicle ids
are `<demand_id>.<index>`.

## Shared definition

- `scenario_id: grid_3x3`
- `warmup_seconds: 300`, `measurement_duration_seconds: 3600`, `step_length: 1.0`
  (total 3900 s; measurement steps `300 <= i < 3900`) - identical for S1-S4
- `turning_ratios`: straight `0.70`, left `0.15`, right `0.15` (approved protocol values)
- `demand_level: MEDIUM`
- `controller_id: B0_FIXED` (default only)
- `communication.delay_steps: 0`, `communication.drop_probability: 0.0`

`controller_id` is a default only. Validation must run at least `B0_FIXED` and
`B2_LOCAL_PRESSURE` as controller overrides for smoke execution.

## S1_BALANCED

Config: `scenarios/grid_3x3/scenario.yaml` (seed 42), per-seed files
`scenario_s1_balanced_seed_<seed>.yaml`. Routes: `routes_s1_seed_<seed>.rou.xml`.

- All 12 border entries inject the same rate (MEDIUM 150 veh/h each, calibrated).
- Every route follows the 70/15/15 walk; no intersection is forced into every route.
- Expected coverage: each of the nine intersections carries about 11% of all junction passes
  (A1 10.9%, A5 10.9%, A9 11.2% for seed 42); no idle corners.
- Expected maximum approach load: 150 veh/h (every approach carries the same
  expected load).

## S2_DIRECTIONAL_PEAK

Config: `scenario_s2_directional_peak.yaml`. Routes: `routes_s2_seed_<seed>.rou.xml`.

- Dominant West -> East profile: the three west border entries (`dominant` group, MEDIUM 320
  veh/h each) inject about 80% of the offered demand; the other nine borders (`residual`,
  320 / 12 = 26.67 veh/h each) inject the remaining 20%.
- The same 70/15/15 walk applies, so straight, left and right movements exist in the dominant
  stream; the 80% share refers to offered demand from the dominant profile, not to vehicles
  travelling straight through the whole network.
- The dominant entries stay below the entry capacity (calibrated so that dominant departure ratio stays >= 0.90) so the offered bias is not hidden
  by entry starvation; `run_metrics.json` reports `offered/departed/arrived` and
  `entry_departure_ratio` by entry and group to verify this at run time.

## S3_DEMAND_SHIFT

Config: `scenario_s3_demand_shift.yaml`. Routes: `routes_s3_seed_<seed>.rou.xml`.

- First half: the S1 balanced base (same 12 entries and rates, from t = 0).
- Second half: the base continues and an abrupt ONE-BORDER (west) shock is added on the three
  west entries (`shock` group, MEDIUM 150 veh/h each, straight-biased 0.90/0.05/0.05) with
  `via_intersection_id: A5`, so shock vehicles are routed through the central intersection.
- The shock starts at 50% of the MEASUREMENT window: `traffic.shock_start_seconds = 2100`
  (`warmup_seconds + measurement_duration_seconds / 2`), 1800 s after the measurement start.
  The scenario validator rejects any other value.
- The base phase equals the calibrated S1 level exactly (stable, no insertion backlog), so the
  shock is visible shortly after it starts instead of being masked by an entry backlog.

## S4_LOCAL_SATURATION

Config: `scenario_s4_local_saturation.yaml`. Routes: `routes_s4_seed_<seed>.rou.xml`.

- Light S1-like background (`base`, MEDIUM 80 veh/h at all 12 borders) plus `hotspot` demand
  (MEDIUM 220 veh/h at all 12 borders, straight-biased 0.90/0.05/0.05) routed through A5
  (`via_intersection_id: A5`).
- Expected share of junction passes: A5 23.0%, neighbours A2/A4/A6/A8 about 13%
  each, corners about 6% each (seed 42). A5's four incoming approaches carry an expected
  740.173 veh/h, above the nominal approach capacity, so congestion concentrates around
  A5 and can propagate to A2/A4/A6/A8. Capacity is not modified artificially and the scenario
  does not require a total deadlock.

## Reproducibility

For each generated route file, the adjacent `*.metadata.json` records:

- `seed`, `demand_profile`, `demand_level`, `shock_start_seconds`, `schedule`;
- `routing` (`turning_ratio_walk`) and the `entries` (rate, window, effective turning ratios,
  `via_intersection_id`, group);
- `turning`: configured ratios, expected proportions, and the REALIZED movement counts and
  proportions (also by group);
- `offered`: offered vehicle counts (total, in the measurement window, by entry, by group,
  by demand item);
- `coverage`: junction passes and shares per intersection;
- `expected_approach_load_veh_per_hour` and its maximum;
- `config_hash`, `scenario_hash`, `route_hash`, `network_hash`.

The same seed and scenario config recreate byte-identical route files (`route_hash`).

## Validation Procedure

For every scenario file:

1. Regenerate with `uv run python scripts/generate_scenarios.py` and confirm `git status`
   shows no unexpected change (deterministic seed).
2. Validate route artifacts (`validate_route_artifacts`: hashes, entry windows, vehicle counts).
3. Confirm realized movement proportions are within 0.03 of the configured ratios.
4. Confirm the structural expectations above (S1 coverage, S2 80% dominant share, S3 shock
   timing and routing, S4 A5 concentration); `tests/test_route_walk.py` checks them.
5. Run a smoke simulation with `B0_FIXED`, `B2_LOCAL_PRESSURE` and a cooperative controller
   (`tests/test_pipeline_sumo.py`).
